## 🔒 Executive Summary
Scrola is dedicated to digital wellbeing and strictly protects user privacy:
- **Zero Data Sale:** We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.
- **No Behavioral Ads:** We do not integrate third-party advertising or tracker SDKs.
- **Private & Local:** All accessibility processing runs 100% on-device in real-time RAM and is discarded immediately.
- **Guest by Default:** No account is needed to block reels, set limits, or evolve your Brainy companion.

---

## 1. Android Accessibility Service API Prominent Disclosure

Scrola requires the **Android Accessibility Service API** solely to enable its core digital wellbeing features: detecting when vertical short-form video feeds are active and enforcing user-set screen time limits.

### ✅ What Scrola Interacts With:
1. Detects when supported apps (Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, Facebook, Snapchat, X, Reddit) are in the foreground.
2. Identifies the UI node identifiers representing short-form video feeds (such as Reels or Shorts viewers).
3. Detects vertical swipe gestures to count reels watched during the day.

### 🚫 What Scrola NEVER Accesses or Collects:
- **NO** personal text messages, direct messages, or emails.
- **NO** passwords, PINs, or financial/banking credentials.
- **NO** photos, videos, or camera recordings.
- **NO** search queries or web browsing history.
- **NO** keystroke logging.

### 🛡️ Real-Time On-Device Processing
All detection happens ephemerally in volatile memory (RAM) on your physical Android device. Screen content is never recorded, captured, saved, or uploaded to any external server. You can disable or revoke this permission at any time in **Android Settings > Accessibility > Scrola**.

---

## 2. Information We Collect and Process

### Local Storage Only (On Your Device):
- Daily reel count and remaining limit.
- Active focus session records.
- Brainy companion XP, current level, streak, and unlocked skins.
- Saved user preferences (overlay position, haptic feedback intensity, schedules).

### Cloud Storage (Optional Account Users Only):
If you voluntarily create a cloud account (via Google Firebase Authentication):
- Email address and username.
- Syncable gamification state (streak days, Brainy level, total XP).
*Guest users store zero data in the cloud.*

### Payments & In-App Purchases:
All subscriptions and purchases (Monthly, Yearly, Lifetime) are processed securely through the **Google Play Billing API**. Scrola never handles or stores your payment details or credit card numbers.

---

## 3. Data Retention, Export & User Deletion Rights

In full compliance with Google Play Data Safety policies:
- **Export Data:** You can export your full daily tracking history to CSV at any time from Settings > Export My Data.
- **Reset Stats:** You can wipe local tracking stats in Settings > Reset Local Data.
- **Delete Account & All Data:** You can permanently purge your cloud account, authentication credentials, and all associated database records directly in Settings > Delete Account, or by emailing `yousfulstudio@gmail.com` with the subject **"Scrola App"**.
