Your Privacy & Data Rights

In full compliance with Google Play's User Data & Account Deletion Policy, Scrola gives every user complete authority to delete their account and associated personal data at any time — either directly within the app or online via email.

How to Request Account & Data Deletion

You can delete your account and personal data using either of the following two methods:

Method 1: In-App Instant Deletion (Recommended)

If you currently have the Scrola app installed on your Android device:

  1. Open the Scrola application.
  2. Navigate to the Profile tab (bottom right icon).
  3. Tap on Privacy & Security (or Privacy Controls).
  4. Scroll to the Data Management section.
  5. Tap Delete Account & Cloud Data.
  6. Confirm your choice by tapping Permanently Delete.

Your Firebase authentication record, profile, and cloud statistics are deleted immediately.

Method 2: Email Request (If App is Uninstalled)

If you have uninstalled Scrola or cannot access the application, submit an email deletion request to our support team:

  • To: yousfulstudio@gmail.com
  • Subject: Scrola App - Delete Account
  • Body Details: Please include the registered email address used in Scrola.

What Data is Deleted vs. Retained

Data Type Status Details & Retention Period
Account Credentials (Email, User ID) DELETED Permanently deleted from Firebase Auth immediately.
Cloud Stats & Game Progress (XP, Streaks, Skins) DELETED Permanently purged from Cloud Firestore immediately.
Local Usage Metrics (Reel counters, timers) DELETED Erased when in-app deletion is triggered, or on app uninstall.
Google Play In-App Purchase Records RETAINED Google Play Billing token history is retained by Google for accounting, tax, and refund compliance as required by applicable laws (up to 90 days or statutory period). Scrola does not store credit card details.

Deletion Processing Timeline

  • In-App Deletion: Processed instantaneously in real time upon confirmation.
  • Email Requests: Verified and executed within 7 business days. A confirmation email will be sent once deletion is complete.

Guest Mode Users

If you used Scrola without creating an account (Guest Mode), your reel tracking statistics are stored 100% locally on your device. No data was ever transmitted to our cloud servers. You can clear your local data anytime in the app under Settings > Reset Local Data, or simply by clearing the app data in Android system settings or uninstalling the app.
