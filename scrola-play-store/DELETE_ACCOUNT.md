## 1. Overview & Google Play Compliance
In full compliance with Google Play's User Data & Account Deletion Policy, Scrola gives every user complete authority to delete their account and associated personal data at any time — either directly within the mobile application or online via email.

---

## 2. How to Request Account & Data Deletion

### Method 1: In-App Instant Deletion (Recommended)
If you have the Scrola app installed on your Android device:
1. Open the **Scrola** application.
2. Tap the **Profile** tab in the bottom navigation bar.
3. Select **Privacy & Security** (or **Privacy Controls**).
4. Under the **Data Management** section, tap **Delete Account & Cloud Data**.
5. Confirm by tapping **Permanently Delete**.

> **Result:** Your account record, authentication token, profile info, and cloud synchronization statistics are purged from Google Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore immediately.

---

### Method 2: Email Deletion Request (If App is Uninstalled)
If you uninstalled the application or cannot access your device, submit a deletion request to our support team:
* **To:** `yousfulstudio@gmail.com`
* **Subject:** `Scrola App - Delete Account`
* **Body:** Please specify the registered email address associated with your Scrola account.

*Our team will process the request and permanently purge your records within **7 business days**, sending you a confirmation reply once finalized.*

| **Account Credentials** (Email, User ID) | **Permanently Deleted** | Erased from Firebase Auth immediately. |

## 4. Guest Mode Users
If you used Scrola without creating an account (Guest Mode), your data was **never sent to any cloud server** — it resides 100% locally on your device in private app storage. You can wipe this data at any moment by tapping **Reset Local Data** in the app's settings or clearing the app storage in your Android settings.
